# About Me

Howdy! I am a electronics and computer hobbyist who was a teaching assistant for a class that used the LC3 programming language. I did this for a couple years and really enjoyed my experience helping students learn. I had a set of powerpoints I would show to students to supplement the material but always wanted to have a single source to point them to for all of their questions. That is what I hope this blog will one day be!

As with most things in college, you start off with big side project aspirations and slowly over time abandon them completely. Especially as post graduation life kicks in. Life is busy and I move very slowly with updating content, but I will be updating content for as long as I am capable!
