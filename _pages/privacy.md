# Privacy Policy

## Introduction
Your privacy is important to me. This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, and protect your information when you visit my blog.

## Information I Collect
- **Google Analytics Data**: I use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with my blog. This includes information such as:
  - Pages visited
  - Time spent on the site
  - Browser type and device information
  - Referring websites
- **Google Ads Data**: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including DoubleClick DART cookies) to serve ads on this site. These cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other websites.
- **Personal Information**: I do not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g., through comments or through contact).

## How I Use Information
- To improve the content and user experience of my blog
- To analyze trends and measure engagement
- To monitor and prevent technical issues
- To display relevant advertisements through Google Ads

## Cookies
- Google Analytics and Google Ads use cookies to collect data. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.
- Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your interests and browsing history.
- You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect your experience on my blog.

## Third-Party Services
- Google Analytics may share data with other Google services.
- Google Ads may use information to personalize advertising content.
- I do not sell or share your information with third parties outside of Google’s services.

## Data Protection
I take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

## Your Choices
- You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
- You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies.

## Changes to This Policy
I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

## Contact Me
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact me at [thelc3tutor@gmail.com](mailto:thelc3tutor@gmail.com).
